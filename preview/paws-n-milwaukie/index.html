Our story

A little shop that just loves dogs.

Paws n' Milwaukie has been a family-owned corner of SE McLoughlin since 2005. We started with a simple idea: give neighbors an easy, affordable place to keep their dogs clean — whether they want to do it themselves or hand the whole job to us.

Two decades later, that hasn't changed. You'll get real people who know your dog's name, take their time, and genuinely care that every pup leaves happy, comfortable, and smelling great.
