The concept

Two great things, one great room.

We combined our two favorite kinds of place — the local record shop and the neighborhood pub — into a single spot where you can crate-dig with a cold one in hand.

The Record Shop

New & used vinyl and tapes

Long bins packed with new releases and used finds across every genre — plus cassettes and vintage stereo gear. We buy used collections, so the racks are always turning over. There are even cupholders on the bins, so your pint comes digging with you.

The Pub

Northwest beer, cider & wine

Seven rotating taps focused on Northwest and seasonal pours, plus canned beer, cider, seltzer, wine and non-alcoholic options — enjoy it at the horseshoe bar or take it to go. Mix-and-match four-packs and $5 mimosas every Saturday & Sunday.

Our story

Born from two loves: records and beer.

The Record Pub came together during 2020, dreamed up by a few friends who loved the local record store and the neighborhood pub in equal measure. So we did the obvious thing — we combined the two.

We opened in the historic Iron Horse Building in Sellwood-Moreland in summer 2022, and it's grown into exactly what we hoped: a place where people flip through records, talk music at the bar, trade tips, and turn into regulars.

It has, as we like to say, surpassed our expectations — and we're just getting started.

— The crew at The Record Pub
