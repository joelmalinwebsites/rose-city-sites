The Shop

An honest chair on NE 15th.

Dillingers is a proper neighborhood barber shop — the kind of place where you can grab a walk-in, talk about your day, and leave looking sharp. No frills, no fuss. Just skilled barbers who take pride in a clean cut.

The shop is led by owner and master barber Patrick Abernethy, alongside a crew of talented barbers who've built a loyal following one great cut at a time. Regulars come back for years — and bring their kids and their dads along with them.

Whether it's a classic cut, a fresh fade, or a hot-towel straight shave, you're in good hands.
