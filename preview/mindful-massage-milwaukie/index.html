Meet Linda

Bodywork guided by intention

Linda Jutzi has called Oregon home since 2001. Over the years, massage carried her through pregnancies, injuries, grief, and growth — and she built Mindful Massage Milwaukie to offer that same steady, healing presence to others.

A graduate of the East West College of the Healing Arts, Linda trained in anatomy, physiology, and pathology alongside Swedish massage, deep tissue, and myofascial release — then went on to advanced work in manual lymph drainage, craniosacral therapy, sound healing, and Reiki. Her belief is simple: touch offered with caring intention can help the body, mind, and spirit heal.
