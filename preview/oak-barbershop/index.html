Our story

Small shop. Steady hands. Since 2011.

Augustus "Gus" Bowman opened Oak in 2011 with a simple idea: a calm, unpretentious room where a good haircut is the whole point — and where you feel like a regular from the moment you sit down.

It's stayed small on purpose. Two chairs, appointments only, no rush and no phone ringing off the hook — just careful work, a hot towel, and time to talk. Gus and Kyle remember your cut and pick up the conversation where you left it.

Tucked into Hosford-Abernethy off SE Division, Oak has quietly become the neighborhood's go-to for a precise, honest cut at a fair price.
