Room for everyone
The Pod

A gathering spot, not just a parking lot.

Chop Chop opened in late 2024 to give outer SE Portland a place to slow down and eat well together — a clean, open pod on the corner of Powell and 174th, behind the Meadowland shops.

Pull into free on-site parking, order from whichever cart smells best, and settle in at the covered picnic tables. There's a play area for the kids and restrooms on-site, so it's an easy call for families, coworkers, and the whole neighborhood.

  • Free on-site parking
  • Covered seating
  • Kids' play area
  • Restrooms on-site
