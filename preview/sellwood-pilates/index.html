Our story

Supporting the Portland Pilates community since 1999.

Sellwood Pilates has been a fixture of the Sellwood-Moreland neighborhood for more than twenty-five years — a full studio built around real, focused attention rather than crowded rooms and rushed reps.

Owner Vicki is a senior-level certified teacher who completed her full-studio training in 2005 and has spent nearly two decades teaching movement in Portland. She's joined by a team of certified teachers and apprentices, each bringing their own path into the work.

"In 10 sessions you will feel different. In 20 you will look different. In 30 you will have a new body." — Joseph Pilates

Whether you're an advanced practitioner or trying the reformer for the very first time, you'll get an environment that makes room for you — and a workout built around your body.
