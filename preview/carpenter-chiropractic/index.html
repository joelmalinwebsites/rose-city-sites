How we can help

Care for everyday aches and real injuries.

Dr. Carpenter uses multiple safe, effective, and gentle techniques to relieve pain — matched to what your body needs.

Neck & Back Pain

Spinal adjustments for neck pain, back, hip, and leg pain — including sciatica relief and everyday stiffness.

Headaches & Migraines

Care for tension headaches and migraines rooted in the neck and upper spine, so you can think clearly again.

TMJ & Jaw Pain

Diagnosis and treatment of TMJ and jaw pain — clicking, tension, and discomfort that make everyday life harder.

Shoulder, Arm & Carpal Tunnel

Relief for shoulder, arm, and hand problems, including carpal tunnel — so you can work and rest comfortably.

Auto & Work Injury

We readily accept auto accident cases and workers' compensation — whiplash and injury care, handled with your paperwork in mind.

Massage Therapy

Therapeutic massage to ease tight muscles and support your adjustments — relaxation with a purpose.

Meet your chiropractor

Dr. Kereah Carpenter, DC

Dr. Carpenter graduated from Western States Chiropractic College in Portland and has cared for patients in the Beaverton and Aloha area since 1992. The clinic is local, family-owned, and operated — and it shows in how patients are treated.

Her approach favors family-oriented, natural alternatives for pain relief, using multiple safe, effective, and gentle techniques matched to each person. The goal is simple: help your body get back in balance, and keep it there.
