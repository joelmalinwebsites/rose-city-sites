Our story

A Gresham fixture since 1984

For four decades, Gresham Bicycle Center has been the shop East County riders come back to — the kind of place where the folks behind the counter actually know bikes, and take the time to explain what yours needs.

Owner Ken Whitney and the crew are known for prompt, honest service at fair prices, without pushing parts you don't need. There's even a little BMX museum inside — a nod to the cycling history this shop has been part of since day one.

Freedom, excitement, and endless possibilities. We build it, you ride it.
