What we do

Traditional at heart, custom by request

We're a traditional shop that works in a wide variety of styles. Bring in a reference or an idea and we'll build it with you — in person.

01

American Traditional

Bold lines, solid color, built to last. Flash off the wall or a custom piece drawn for you.

02

Black & Grey

Smooth shading and clean linework — from small pieces to portraits and larger black-and-grey work.

Rich color, florals, and Japanese-inspired sleeves and back pieces designed to flow with the body.
