The Deep Dig
Our story

Run by an ear you can trust.

Dig Vinyl is a small square with high ceilings, tucked behind a coffee shop in Sellwood — the kind of room that feels like a secret club the moment you walk in.

It's run by Scott Thayer, the DJ behind KMHD's rare-vinyl show, The Deep Dig. The shop's jazz- and soul-focused inventory is curated with the same care as the show — which is why even the $5 bins feel hand-selected.

Scott and the crew are on hand to point you toward the treasure. Come dig.

— Scott & the Dig Vinyl crew
