Since 1972

Half a century of framing Portland.

Chrisman Framing opened its doors in 1972 and has been helping Portlanders frame their photos and artwork ever since. Over more than fifty years, the shop has built a reputation for high-quality work and genuinely personal service.

It is still locally owned and operated by the Chrisman family, and still grounded in the same idea: the right frame doesn't just protect a piece — it gives your art or memorabilia a unique character all its own.

Today that care lives on across four neighborhood shops around the Portland area, each with the same museum-quality materials and the same eye for detail.

— The Chrisman Family
