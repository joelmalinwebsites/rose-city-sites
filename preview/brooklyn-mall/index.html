Our story

A family shop, built from many hands.

Brooklyn Mall began when Julie Evans and her husband Jerry Leffel combined the smaller furniture businesses they'd run — including Julie's teak shop — into one collective. They invited other dealers and consigners to join in, and the result is the eclectic mix you'll wander through today.

That's the charm of the place: handmade furniture, imports, antiques and estate-sale finds all under one roof, each with its own history. Reviewers keep pointing to the same thing — knowledgeable, genuinely friendly folks who care about the pieces they sell.
