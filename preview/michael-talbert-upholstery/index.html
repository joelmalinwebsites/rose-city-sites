What we do

Craftsmanship for every piece

From a beloved family heirloom to a full commercial lobby — we rebuild it right, by hand, to last another generation.

Reupholstery

Sofas, chairs, sectionals, ottomans and dining seats — brought back to better-than-new with fresh foam, springs and fabric.

Antique Restoration

Complete restoration of antique and heirloom furniture — frames, springs, finishes and fabric, with the care old pieces deserve.

Cushions & Foam

New foam, down and cushion inserts that bring back the comfort and shape your furniture had the day you bought it.

Custom & Design Trade

Custom furniture, headboards, benches and pillows built for homeowners and interior designers alike.

Repairs

Torn seams, broken legs, wobbly or cracked frames and worn springs — fixed properly so the piece is solid again.

Fabric & Leather

Thousands of fabrics, leathers, vinyls and eco-friendly recycled options — plus decorative nails, trim and accessories.

Our approach

Furniture worth keeping.

Good furniture was built to last — solid frames, real joinery, pieces with history. Reupholstering what you already own is kinder to your wallet and to the planet than replacing it, and you end up with something far nicer than anything off a showroom floor.

We've been doing this in the Portland area since 1986. Every piece is measured, stripped, rebuilt and finished by hand, in fabric you choose, made to be lived on for years to come.

— Michael Talbert Upholstery
